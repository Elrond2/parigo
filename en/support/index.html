Parigo support

Parigo is an iPhone app. Here are the answers to the most common questions. If yours is not here, write to us.

Where is my data?

On your device. Parigo has no user account and no server of its own where your accounts, transactions or groups are kept, so we cannot see them. The statements you import are read on the device and the imported transactions never leave it. Which technical data is sent to third parties (advertising, subscription, exchange rates) is explained in the privacy policy.

I have forgotten my passphrase

The protection passphrase cannot be recovered: if you forget it, nobody, including us, can open that data. If you saved a private backup and remember the passphrase you encrypted it with, you can open it with that. That is why each private backup should be kept together with its passphrase.

Changing phone

Before you change, make a private backup from Preferences and keep that file together with its passphrase. On the new phone, import it from within the app. Parigo's data is requested to be left out of system backups, so do not count on the phone's automatic backup bringing it across. If you reinstall or lose the phone without a private backup, you lose that data.

How do I cancel the "No ads" subscription?

In the subscription settings of your Apple account, or from Parigo with "Manage subscription": apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. It stays active until the end of the period already paid. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription: it has to be cancelled with Apple.

How do I ask for a refund?

Charges and refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. You can ask at reportaproblem.apple.com; Apple decides under its own rules. Cancelling the renewal is not the same as asking for a refund of a period already charged. More detail in the terms of use.

Restoring purchases

If you have changed phone or reinstalled Parigo, open Preferences → "No ads" and tap "Restore purchases", using the same Apple account you bought with. You do not need to create an account in Parigo. A subscription bought on Apple does not work in other stores.

I have seen an inappropriate ad

Next to each ad there is an "About this ad" button that explains why you see it and lets you report it. Reporting opens your email app with a message addressed to hola@mundotuni.com and sends only what you write. No filter is perfect: if you see an ad that should not appear, tell us.

I want access to my purchase data, or its deletion

The data you keep in Parigo (accounts, transactions, groups) is only on your device and you control it. For the subscription and purchase data that RevenueCat processes, write to hola@mundotuni.com and include your "Support code", which is in Preferences → About, with a button to copy it. Without that code we cannot locate your record.

Why does it ask for my year of birth?

To decide whether Parigo may show you advertising: people under 16 are not shown any. The year is stored only on your device. The question is required to keep using the app.

How do I share a group?

With an invitation link and encrypted files that you send through the channel you prefer. There is no server and no automatic sync: each person sends and receives their changes by hand. To share, you first need to turn on passphrase protection. The invitation contains the group's key: send it only to people taking part, through a private channel.
