Where is my data?

Your financial records (accounts, transactions and groups) are stored on your device. Statements are read locally and you review transactions before saving them. You choose whether to export files or share changes to a group. Advertising, purchase and exchange-rate lookup services are explained in the privacy policy.

I have forgotten my passphrase

The protection passphrase cannot be recovered. If you forget it, we cannot open the data protected with it. You can recover what is in an earlier private backup: an encrypted backup needs its own passphrase, which may differ from the app passphrase; a backup exported without encryption needs no passphrase. Keep backups in a safe place and keep their passphrases.

Changing phone

Before you change, make a private backup from Settings → Backups and restore (in version 1.0, from the “Full private backup” section in Preferences). Keep the file in a safe place and keep its passphrase if it is encrypted.

On the new phone, open Settings → Backups and restore → Restore private backup…; in version 1.0, find “Restore private backup…” in Preferences. You can also restore it from the lock screen. If the backup is encrypted, use its passphrase and follow the steps to set up local protection. Statement import does not restore private backups.

Parigo’s data is requested to be left out of system backups, so do not count on the phone’s automatic backup bringing it across. If you reinstall or lose the phone without a private backup, you lose the data you have not saved in another way.

How do I import my first statement?

In version 1.1, open the Accounts tab: you will see the Transactions screen and its “Import” button. In version 1.0, open the Import tab. Create or choose a local account, select the statement file you downloaded from your bank and review the transactions before saving the rows you choose. Parigo does not connect to your bank; check that it recognises the file format and the account’s currency.

If you have a Parigo private backup instead, follow the backup restoration steps.

How does "No ads" work?

Parigo is free and has all its features. It may show an advertising banner. “No ads” is an optional one-off purchase that removes that banner and nothing else. The price is shown in the store before you buy and is charged once to your Apple account. See the terms of use for details.

How do I ask for a refund?

You can request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. See the terms of use for details.

Restore purchase

If you have changed phone or reinstalled Parigo, open Settings → Remove ads → No ads and tap “Restore purchase”, using the same Apple account you bought with. In version 1.0, open Preferences and find the “No ads” section. You do not need to create an account in Parigo. A purchase made on Apple does not carry over to other stores.

I have seen an inappropriate ad

Next to each ad there is an "About this ad" button that explains why you see it and lets you report it. Reporting opens your email app with a message addressed to soporte@parigo.app and sends only what you write. No filter is perfect: if you see an ad that should not appear, tell us.

I want access to my purchase data, or its deletion

Your financial records are stored on your device; you decide whether to export them or share changes to a group. For the purchase data that RevenueCat processes, write to soporte@parigo.app and include your "Support code", which has a button to copy it. In Settings, scroll to the “About” group and open “Version and legal texts”; in version 1.0, find “About” in Preferences. Without that code we cannot locate your record.

Why does it ask for my year of birth?

To apply advertising options according to age. The year is stored only on your device. The question is required to keep using the app. See the privacy policy for details. If you see an ad that should not appear, contact support.

How do I share a group?

Each person needs the invitation link and the welcome file. Then you send and receive changes by hand in encrypted files, through the channel you prefer. Group changes are not synced automatically. To share, you first need to turn on passphrase protection. The invitation contains the group's key: send it only to people taking part, through a private channel.
