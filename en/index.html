What Parigo does

Your money at a glance

Total balance across your accounts and how it has changed over recent months, plus this month's spending by category with the exact list below. Each currency is kept on its own: euros are never added to dollars.

Budgets and goals

Set a monthly budget, overall or by category, and see how much you have used and how much is left. Record contributions to a savings goal with an amount and a date.

Analysis

Monthly bars with the average of closed months, income versus spending and a breakdown by category. Tap a bar or a category to see the transactions behind it.

Statements, without a bank connection

Download the statement from your bank's website or app and import it as a file (CSV, Excel or HTML). It is read on your device, you review every transaction before saving it and you can see possible duplicates. Parigo does not connect to banks.

Group expenses

Note what each person pays in a flat, on a trip or as a couple, with an equal or unequal split. See each person's balance and "who owes whom". Includes recurring costs, such as rent, and a joint account.

Sharing, by hand and encrypted

Invite each person with a link and a welcome file. Then send changes in encrypted files through the channel you prefer. Group changes are not synced automatically: each person sends and receives their changes by hand. To share, you first need to turn on passphrase protection.

Your financial records, on your device

  • Your accounts, transactions and groups are stored on your device. You choose whether to export files or share changes to a group.
  • Optional protection with a passphrase that only you know. If you forget it, it cannot be recovered. An earlier backup may help you recover what it contains; see the frequently asked questions.
  • Statements are read on your device and you review transactions before saving them.
  • The advertising banner does not use your transactions, amounts, banks or names. See the privacy policy for how advertising data is handled.
  • If you pay in another currency, Parigo can look up that day's exchange rate. It only sends the date and the currency codes, never amounts or descriptions, and you can turn it off.

Advertising, purchase and exchange-rate lookup services are explained in the privacy policy.

No ads

Optional one-off payment

Parigo does not move money, does not connect to banks and does not give financial advice. The amounts are the ones you note down or import. Available in Spanish and English.
