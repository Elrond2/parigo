Budgets and shared expenses, on your iPhone.

Your accounts, your budgets and the shared costs of your flat or trip, in one place. No sign-up: your data stays on your device and Parigo never connects to your bank.

What Parigo does

Your money at a glance

Total balance across your accounts and how it has changed over recent months, plus this month's spending by category with the exact list below. Each currency is kept on its own: euros are never added to dollars.

Budgets and goals

Set a monthly budget, overall or by category, and see how much you have used and how much is left. Record contributions to a savings goal with an amount and a date.

Analysis

Monthly bars with the average of closed months, income versus spending and a breakdown by category. Tap a bar or a category to see the transactions behind it.

Statements, without a bank connection

Download the statement from your bank's website or app and import it as a file (CSV, Excel or HTML). It is read on your device, you review every transaction before saving it and you can see possible duplicates. Parigo does not connect to banks.

Group expenses

Note what each person pays in a flat, on a trip or as a couple, with an equal or unequal split. See each person's balance and "who owes whom". Includes recurring costs, such as rent, and a joint account.

Sharing, by hand and encrypted

Share a group with an invitation link and send changes in encrypted files through the channel you prefer. There is no server and no automatic sync: each person sends and receives their changes by hand. To share, you first need to turn on passphrase protection.

Your data, on your device

  • Your accounts, transactions and groups are stored on your device. There is no account to create and no server of our own where they are kept.
  • Optional protection with a passphrase that only you know. If you forget it, there is no way to recover it or to open that data.
  • Transactions imported from a statement never leave the device.
  • The advertising banner does not use your transactions, amounts, banks or names. Under-16s are not shown advertising.
  • If you pay in another currency, Parigo can look up that day's exchange rate. It only sends the date and the currency codes, never amounts or descriptions, and you can turn it off.

The full details are in the privacy policy.

Price

Free

€0

Parigo is free to use and does not charge for noting down more transactions. It shows a discreet advertising banner on Home, Analysis and Groups.

No ads

€0.99 a month or €7.99 a year

An optional subscription that removes the advertising banner; it unlocks no features. These are the base prices for Spain: in other countries Apple sets them.

It renews automatically every month or every year and is charged to your Apple account. You can cancel at any time in the subscription settings of your Apple account, and it stays active until the end of the period already paid. More information in the terms of use.

Parigo does not move money, does not connect to banks and does not give financial advice. The amounts are the ones you note down or import. Available in Spanish and English.
