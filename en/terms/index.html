It is not a financial or payment service, does not connect to banks, does not hold funds and does not give financial advice. It presents what you note down or import; the balances and splits it shows are calculations made with that data, not payments. Debts between people are paid outside Parigo, and the decisions you take with what you see are yours. Always check that what you noted down is correct. Parigo runs on an iPhone with the version of iOS stated on Parigo's App Store page.
